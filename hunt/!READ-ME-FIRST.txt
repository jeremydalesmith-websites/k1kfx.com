FOX PURSUIT - START HERE
========================

1. Unzip everything in this package into one folder, keeping the
   structure as it is (including the maps folder).

2. Open SETUP.txt in Notepad, or any plain-text editor. It is the
   complete guide, and it assumes no prior knowledge.

3. Allow about an hour and a half. Most of that is creating a free
   Firebase project; the app's own configuration takes ten minutes.


WHAT IS IN HERE
  SETUP.txt              the guide - read this first
  0-config-*.js          the four files you personalize (Section 4)
  everything else        the app itself - do not edit


BEFORE YOU START you will need somewhere to publish the files - any
web host that serves HTTPS, or Firebase's free hosting - and a free
Google account. SETUP.txt Section 1 covers both, and helps you choose.
