This phone can’t check its own compass, so it won’t warn you when the reading is off. Before taking bearings, step away from vehicles and metal and wave the phone in a wide figure-8, turning as you go. Then point it in a direction you know and check that the compass agrees, or check it with a hand compass. For the best bearings, walk 60–90 ft straight toward the signal; the compass line at the lower left of the map changes to “GPS heading” once GPS is supplying the bearing. To check the compass, keep pointing the same way when you stop: after a few seconds the line switches back to the compass, and the two readings should agree.
