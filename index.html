Amateur Radio · Lincoln, NE

K1KFX

1How it works

Fox frequencies are revealed on K0KKV at the starting time. Log your bearings in the app and radio them to net control.

2Step by step

  1. Start Hunting → sign in → Create Hunt.
  2. Turn on tracking (GPS + compass).
  3. Point at the signal, tap Submit.
  4. Radio your location and bearing to net control.
  5. Found it? Close the hunt, start a new one.
Optional: buddy up One hunt, you both join it, one shared map.

3Tips

  • Name your hunt call + fox: K1KFX-1
  • 8 or fewer good bearings for best results.
  • Figure-8 the phone to calibrate; avoid cars and metal.
  • Offline map: Download on Wi-Fi before leaving.
