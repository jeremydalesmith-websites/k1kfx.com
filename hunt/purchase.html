Which one?

Team is for a club or organization hosting fox hunts. Members can use the application as individuals competing against each other, or multiple members can collaborate with everyone's bearings appearing on everyone's map, without any need for a net control. Solo is for one person: your bearings stay on your phone and nothing is shared. Both use the same maps and the same triangulation. A Team license does not include Solo; a Solo license does not run a club site.
