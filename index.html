1How the hunt works

Net control and the fox frequencies are given out at the start of each hunt. You record your own bearings on your phone with the Fox Pursuit app and radio them in to net control.

2Your hunt, step by step

  1. Tap Join a Hunt, sign in with your callsign, and create your own hunt
  2. Turn on tracking and let your phone's GPS and compass fill in your location and bearing.
  3. When you're ready, a single tap on submit records both.
  4. Report your location and bearing to net control.
  5. Keep chasing on your own map. When you find the fox, close that hunt and start a new one for the next fox.
Optional: buddy up Hunting with a friend? Create one hunt and have your buddy join it by name. You'll share one map with both sets of bearings.

3Tips

  • Name your hunt after yourself and the fox, such as K1KFX-1, so it doesn't collide with anyone else's.
  • Fewer, good bearings beat many. About 10 or fewer keeps the map useful.
  • Calibrate the compass by waving the phone in a figure-8, and stay away from cars and metal.
  • Download an offline map on Wi-Fi before you leave. Tap the map to set the center, then Download.
