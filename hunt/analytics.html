Definitions

Summary metrics

Hunts
The total number of hunts STARTED, all-time. Counted when a hunt is created, so it includes hunts that were abandoned without being closed - those contribute nothing to the other figures below, which are recorded when a hunt is closed. This number will therefore always be the largest.
Participations
The total count of people-in-hunts across every hunt. One person joining three hunts counts as three participations.
Compass
How many bearings, all-time, were taken with the device's built-in compass.
GPS
How many bearings, all-time, came from GPS heading — your direction of travel while moving.
Manual
How many bearings, all-time, were entered by hand — typed in or set by tapping the map.
Total Bearings
Every bearing submitted, all-time, from all sources combined (compass + GPS + manual).
Formed an Estimate
The share of hunts in which enough bearings crossed to produce a probable fox location. Higher means hunts more often converged on an answer.
Avg Hunt Length
The average time a hunt stayed open, from creation to close, in minutes.
Avg Participants / Hunt
The average number of distinct people who took part in each hunt.

Per-hunt record columns

Date
The date the hunt closed. No time of day is recorded.
People
How many distinct people took part in that hunt.
Bearings
Total bearings submitted in that hunt (compass + GPS + manual).
Cmp
Bearings from the compass in that hunt.
GPS
Bearings from GPS heading in that hunt.
Man
Bearings entered by hand — typed or map-tapped — in that hunt.
Mins
How many minutes the hunt was open.
Est
Whether that hunt's bearings crossed to produce a probable fox location (yes / no).
Close
How the hunt ended: "auto" (reached its time limit) or "manual" (the creator closed it).
iOS/And/Desk
A count of that hunt's submissions by device type — iOS / Android / Desktop.
Location (coarse)
The general area of the hunt, deliberately blurred to roughly a 12.5-mile radius. It never shows an exact location.
Build
The app version that recorded the hunt.
